## Limitations

PLACO+ tests variant-level statistical pleiotropy and cannot distinguish biological, horizontal, or mediated pleiotropy. The analysis is based on public summary statistics, CAT has Finnish-European ancestry, and LD clumping is a discovery-organization step rather than causal-variant evidence.
