# Phase 3A Claim Hierarchy

Date: 2026-09-21 (Asia/Shanghai)

The manuscript should keep the following evidence hierarchy frozen.

## Level 1 — global architecture

Global LDSC estimates describe genome-wide genetic correlation between an ocular and a systemic trait. They do not determine whether local regions share the same direction.

## Level 2 — local covariance architecture

SUPERGNOVA identifies atlas-wide FDR-significant local genetic covariance regions and their rho direction. These are local statistical associations, not shared causal variants.

## Level 3 — independent locus convergence

Independent PLACO loci provide locus-level support within the validated Phase 1D regions. The primary Phase 3A units are 58 independent loci in 41 supported pair-region entries, with 41/41 lead-locus directional concordance. This supports a directional local-sharing architecture under the frozen statistical plan.

## Level 4 — supportive functional interpretation

VEP, positional gene mapping, xQTL lookup, recurrent-region summaries, and direction-specific pathway analyses are descriptive/supportive evidence. They may prioritize biological themes for follow-up, but they do not identify causal genes or causal variants.

## Level 5 — not established

The following claims are not established by this project:

- a shared causal variant;
- a shared causal gene;
- a molecular mechanism;
- confirmed antagonistic pleiotropy;
- clinical utility or therapeutic relevance.

The public-LD fine-mapping route remains closed after the Phase 2B-R technical gate. Functional annotations must not be used to reopen that route or override the frozen LD decision.
